# Design process

A skill that runs design work from a question to build-ready issues: rounds of three directions on a Design canvas, reviewed and scored, then one issue per screen.

## Work

**Owner**:
The person who steers the design by picking a direction each round, and who owns every product and taste call.
_Avoid_: User (that is the product's end user), client

**Round**:
One decision, made by drawing three directions on the same screens and asking the owner to pick. A round never reopens an earlier one.
_Avoid_: Iteration, pass, version

**Direction**:
One coherent answer to a round's question, drawn at equal depth with the other two.
_Avoid_: Option, variant, concept

**Board**:
One screen or concept drawn as a live HTML file on the canvas, scored out of 7 by two reviewers.
_Avoid_: Mockup, frame, artboard

**Canon**:
The one vocabulary, data set and pattern list every board in a piece of work follows.
_Avoid_: Style guide, spec

**Review tile**:
A readable 864x720 slice of a rendered board. Reviewers and drafters read tiles and contact sheets, never whole boards.
_Avoid_: Thumbnail, screenshot

## Roles

Each role is its own agent with its own model and effort, named only in `bindings/`: explorer, researcher, drafter, fixer, reviewer, second reviewer and ticket drafter. The drafter stays alive for its own fix passes. A fixer applies a fix list only when the drafter is gone.
