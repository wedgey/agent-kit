# Lanes are a role agent, paced by the queue

On 2026-11-10 one epic used about 75% of two five-hour usage windows in a row, and both windows hit the limit. A lane was a general-purpose agent with its tier passed on each dispatch, and the orchestrator passed the frontier model to 11 of 27 lanes, 9 of them with no effort. Those lanes were 29% of the first window. Lanes loaded the whole conductor skill 43 times and a design skill 32 times, and spawned 96 implementers, though a lane is meant to write its ticket itself. Verifiers ran at frontier, about three per PR, and were the largest cost of the second window at 22%. Up to 11 lanes and 16 role agents ran at once against an e2e queue with one slot, so lanes sat parked for up to 12 minutes and each idle agent paid to write its whole cached context again on its next turn: 68 such writes cost about 20% of a window. When the usage limit cut 24 agents off mid-turn, the orchestrator dispatched fresh lanes for them after the reset, which redid their work instead of continuing it. Delegates ran to 230k to 323k tokens of context under the 250k limit of ADR 0011.

So the Claude Code binding ships the lane as a role agent, `conductor:lane`, pinned to strong-code and keeping the Agent tool. A probe on v2.1.322 showed a plugin agent can spawn delegates. The agent carries the lane's costly rules and a reading list of sections, so a lane no longer loads whole skills, and a frontier lane needs the model and effort passed together. A lane writes its main chain itself and spawns implementers only for side work that runs in parallel with its own. A verifier runs once per PR, at merge-ready, at strong-code when its check is a named spec and its screenshots, and at frontier when it judges, as the epic's own verifier does. The epic dispatches every ready ticket, but after the first wave holds the next dispatch while a queue its lanes use has a waiter and one of its lanes is still working, which replaces the fixed lane count of ADR 0010. A lane cut off by a usage limit is continued after the reset, never dispatched fresh, and every lane pushes at each stage's end so a cut-off loses at most one stage. The context limit for every delegate drops to 120k, which amends ADR 0011. The lane agent keeps the Agent tool, which amends ADR 0006's rule that no role agent spawns its own delegates.
